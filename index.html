Teeolud — Privacy Policy

App: Teeolud (Android) · Effective date: 07.11.2026 · Last updated: 07.11.2026

Teeolud shows the state of the roads in Estonia, Finland, Lithuania and Latvia right now — road surface temperature and condition, road cameras and road events — from the open data of these countries' road administrations. This policy explains what data the app handles, what stays on your device and which servers the app connects to.

1. Summary

  • Teeolud has no account and no sign-up. It shows no ads and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools. The developer does not collect personal data.
  • Your settings, pinned corridors, pinned station and favourite stations are stored only on your device.
  • If you allow location, the app uses your approximate location while it is open, to find the road weather stations and cameras near you. This happens only on your device: the app asks its server for the data of whole countries, not of a place, and your location is never sent anywhere. Only the identifier of the nearest station is saved on your device, for the widget.
  • The app downloads public road data from its own server, map tiles from OpenFreeMap and camera images from the road administrations. These requests carry nothing about you beyond what every internet connection carries: your IP address. The Teeolud server's host keeps technical logs of requests — IP address, time, the address of the request — for up to 7 days (section 3).

5. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare hosts the Teeolud servers (section 3 and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy).
  • OpenFreeMap provides the map (section 3, openfreemap.org).
  • Your phone's location service determines the approximate location. On most phones it is provided by Google Play services and works under your device's location settings and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play distributes the app and gives the developer aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as installs and crashes) under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • The road data are public open data of Transpordiamet (Estonia), Fintraffic (Finland), Lietuvos automobilių kelių direkcija (Lithuania) and Latvijas Valsts ceļi (Latvia). No data about you is sent to them, except your IP address when the app loads a camera image directly (section 3).

6. Data sharing and sale

Teeolud does not sell, share or transfer any data about you. It does not have any to share.

7. Data retention

Everything the app stores stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app; downloaded road data older than two days are deleted automatically. The developer has no copy and cannot access, restore or delete it for you. Cloudflare's technical logs (section 3) are deleted after at most 7 days.

8. Children

Teeolud is a road conditions app for drivers, not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

9. Security

App data is kept in Android app-private storage, which other apps cannot read. All network connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

10. Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on the developer's side; your data on the device is under your control in the app and in Android settings. If you write to the developer, your email is used only to reply and is deleted when no longer needed. If you are in the EEA and believe your data protection rights have been infringed, you can contact the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon) or the authority of your country.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page is updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes.

Teeolud — политика конфиденциальности

Приложение: Teeolud (Android) · Действует с: 07.11.2026 · Обновлено: 07.11.2026

Teeolud показывает состояние дорог Эстонии, Финляндии, Литвы и Латвии прямо сейчас — температуру и состояние покрытия, дорожные камеры и события на дорогах — по открытым данным дорожных ведомств этих стран. Здесь описано, какие данные обрабатывает приложение, что остаётся на вашем устройстве и к каким серверам оно обращается.

1. Коротко

  • В Teeolud нет аккаунта и регистрации. Нет рекламы, аналитики, сбора отчётов о сбоях и трекеров. Разработчик не собирает личные данные.
  • Настройки, закреплённые коридоры, закреплённая станция и избранные станции хранятся только на вашем устройстве.
  • Если вы разрешите геолокацию, приложение, пока оно открыто, использует ваше приблизительное местоположение, чтобы найти дорожные станции и камеры рядом. Это происходит только на устройстве: у сервера приложение запрашивает данные целых стран, а не точки, и местоположение никуда не отправляется. На устройстве сохраняется только идентификатор ближайшей станции — для виджета.
  • Приложение загружает открытые дорожные данные со своего сервера, тайлы карты с OpenFreeMap и снимки камер с серверов дорожных ведомств. Эти запросы не несут о вас ничего, кроме того, что несёт любое интернет-соединение: ваш IP-адрес. Хостинг сервера Teeolud хранит технические журналы запросов — IP-адрес, время, адрес запроса — до 7 дней (раздел 3).

Teeolud on teede olukorra rakendus juhtidele, mitte lastele suunatud. See ei kogu isikuandmeid kelleltki, ka mitte lastelt.

9. Turvalisus

Rakenduse andmeid hoitakse Androidi rakenduse privaatses salvestusruumis, mida teised rakendused lugeda ei saa. Kõik võrguühendused on krüpteeritud (HTTPS).

10. Teie õigused

Kuna arendajal pole teie isikuandmeid, pole tema poolel midagi vaadata, parandada ega kustutada; seadmes olevad andmed on teie kontrolli all rakenduses ja Androidi seadetes. Kui kirjutate arendajale, kasutatakse teie e-posti aadressi ainult vastamiseks ja see kustutatakse, kui seda enam vaja pole. Kui olete EMP-s ja leiate, et teie andmekaitseõigusi on rikutud, võite pöörduda Andmekaitse Inspektsiooni (aki.ee) või oma riigi järelevalveasutuse poole.

11. Muudatused

Kui rakendus hakkab andmeid käsitlema teisiti, uuendatakse seda lehte enne sellise versiooni avaldamist ja ülal olev kuupäev muutub.
